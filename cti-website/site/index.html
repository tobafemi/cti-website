Why trust CTI

Trust is the currency of trade

CTI turns what's happening on the ground into evidence a UK buyer or investor can check. Every listing is built on three layers.

Layer 1

Visual proof

Geo-tagged, time-stamped video of sites, stock, farmland and plant. You see the asset, not just a document about it.

Layer 2

Data rooms

Accounts, ownership papers and ESG records, organised in a structure UK lawyers and advisers recognise.

Layer 3

Upfront checks

Company registration, who really owns the business and sanctions screening, all before anything is listed.

Everything is presented against English common law, UK ESG frameworks and recognised governance standards.

How we work

Built with chambers and trade bodies, for their members

CTI works alongside chambers of commerce, trade associations and industry bodies in the UK and across Africa. They help vet the opportunities on the platform, and their members get a trusted route into new markets.

  • UK bilateral chambers of commerce
  • African national chambers
  • Trade and industry associations
  • Investment promotion agencies

A trade mission lasts a week.
CTI is open all year.

Chambers and trade bodies can offer CTI to their members under their own name: a year-round, digital trade mission across every sector.

  1. For the trade body

    A valuable new service for members, a share in what it earns, and a say in how opportunities in your market are checked.

  2. For its members

    Access to verified opportunities and introductions, through an organisation they already trust.

  3. For investors

    An independent check on every listing from people who know the local market.
