Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Connect Trade and Invest Global Ltd ("CTI", "we") is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17598282. We are the controller of the personal information described here. You can contact us at hello@ctinvestglobal.com.

What we collect

When you use the form on this website we collect your name, email address, what you're interested in and anything else you choose to give us, such as your job title, organisation, country and message.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you and, if you asked, send you updates about the platform or our profile deck. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or your consent where you've asked for updates.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for automated decision-making.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the website and email:

  • Vercel, which hosts this website and processes form submissions.
  • Resend, which sends the emails generated by the form.
  • Zoho, which hosts our email inbox.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless we start working together, in which case our contract terms apply.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent for updates at any time. Email hello@ctinvestglobal.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies.
