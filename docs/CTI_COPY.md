## 4. Why it matters (`#why`)

**Eyebrow:** Why now

**Heading:** Africa is changing faster than perceptions of it

**Body:**

The African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) is bringing 54 countries together into a single market. The continent holds a large share of the world's critical minerals, huge agricultural potential and the youngest workforce of any region.

Yet UK capital still moves slowly. Outdated risk perceptions and a lack of reliable, first-hand information keep good opportunities off the table.

CTI closes that gap. Short, verified videos give decision-makers in London a direct view of projects and businesses, so conversations start from evidence.

**Figures:**

| Figure | Label |
|--------|-------|
| 54 | countries in the AfCFTA |
| 1.3bn+ | people in a single market |
| ~$3.4tn | combined GDP |

**Source line:** Source: World Bank, *The African Continental Free Trade Area: Economic and Distributional Effects* (2020).

## 5. How we build trust (`#principles`)

**Eyebrow:** Our principles

**Heading:** How we build trust

**Visual transparency**
*Seeing is believing*
Drone footage of site progress, ministerial pitches and 60-second SME spotlights replace static 100-page prospectuses. Investors get a clearer picture earlier, which shortens the first stage of due diligence.

**Country by country**
*54 markets, one framework*
We work with each country individually, while connecting into the tariff-free trade corridors created by the AfCFTA. Investors can build country strategies within a continental view.

**UK governance**
*City of London rigour*
Opportunities are presented against English common law, UK ESG frameworks and recognised governance standards, giving UK capital a familiar, lower-risk route in.

## 7. How it works (`#how`)

> ⚠️ Tobi: check these steps match how the platform actually works before launch.

**Eyebrow:** How it works

**Heading:** From first look to first conversation

**For investors**
1. **Tell us what you're looking for.** Sectors, countries, ticket size and timelines.
2. **Review vetted opportunities.** Watch verified video and read structured summaries for each one.
3. **Request an introduction.** Move to a secure deal room to take the conversation further.

**For businesses**
1. **Apply and get vetted.** Checks are carried out with partner chambers of commerce.
2. **Record your video profile.** We guide you through a 60 to 90 second video of your operations.
3. **Get matched.** Meet UK investors and buyers who are interested in what you do.
