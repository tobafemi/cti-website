Who we are

This notice explains how Connect Trade and Invest Global Ltd ("CTI", "we", "us") uses personal information collected through this website. We are the controller of that information.

Connect Trade and Invest Global Ltd is registered in England and Wales, company number 17598282. Our registered office is 20 Wenlock Road, London, N1 7GU.

If you have any questions about this notice or how we handle your information, email us at hello@ctinvestglobal.com.

What we collect

When you fill in the form on our website, we collect:

  • your name and email address
  • your job title, organisation and country, if you give them
  • what you're interested in, and anything you write in your message
  • whether you've asked to receive updates about the CTI launch

If you email us directly, we'll have your email address and whatever you include in your message.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies on this website. If you choose a colour option on the site, your browser stores that choice locally so the site remembers it. That information stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

We won't sell your information, and we won't use it for anything unrelated to why you gave it to us.

Who we share it with

Your information is only seen by the CTI team and by service providers who help us run the website and our email, such as our website hosting and email providers. They act on our instructions and aren't allowed to use your information for their own purposes.

We won't introduce you to, or share your details with, any other business, investor or trade body unless you've asked us to.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our legal rights.

International transfers

Some of our service providers may store or process information outside the UK. When that happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as a UK adequacy decision or the standard contractual clauses approved for use in the UK.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and related correspondence: up to two years after we were last in touch, unless we start working together, in which case we keep records for as long as the relationship lasts and for a reasonable time afterwards.
  • Launch updates: until you unsubscribe or ask us to stop.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that's wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it
  • ask us to send it to you or another organisation in a usable format
  • withdraw your consent to updates at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing us

To use any of these rights, email hello@ctinvestglobal.com. We'll reply within one month. Some rights have limits, and we'll explain if one applies.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK regulator for data protection, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

We'll update this notice as the CTI platform develops. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
