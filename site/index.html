What we do

A clearer view of African opportunity

CTI is a trade and investment platform linking the UK and Africa. We give investors, businesses and governments one place to find, assess and act on opportunities, backed by verified video rather than long paper prospectuses.

Why now

Africa is changing faster than perceptions of it

The African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) is bringing 54 countries together into a single market. The continent holds a large share of the world's critical minerals, huge agricultural potential and the youngest workforce of any region.

Yet UK capital still moves slowly. Outdated risk perceptions and a lack of reliable, first-hand information keep good opportunities off the table.

CTI closes that gap. Short, verified videos give decision-makers in London a direct view of projects and businesses, so conversations start from evidence.

Our principles

How we build trust

Visual transparency

Seeing is believing

Drone footage of site progress, ministerial pitches and 60-second SME spotlights replace static 100-page prospectuses. Investors get a clearer picture earlier, which shortens the first stage of due diligence.

Country by country

54 markets, one framework

We work with each country individually, while connecting into the tariff-free trade corridors created by the AfCFTA. Investors can build country strategies within a continental view.

UK governance

City of London rigour

Opportunities are presented against English common law, UK ESG frameworks and recognised governance standards, giving UK capital a familiar, lower-risk route in.

The platform

Two tiers, one platform

CTI serves government-to-government projects and commercial deals in separate tiers, each designed for its audience.

Sovereign tier · G2G

For governments and national institutions

Ministerial engagement, national infrastructure and sovereign assets.

  • Country showrooms. Dedicated visual hubs for national development banks and investment promotion agencies.

  • Blended finance modelling. Show how projects could combine public and private finance, including UK development finance structures.

  • Secure deal rooms. Encrypted spaces for confidential bilateral discussions.

Sectors
  • Infrastructure
  • Renewable energy
  • Rail and transport
  • Critical minerals

Commercial tier · B2B

For growing businesses and investors

Mid-market expansion, trade corridors and high-growth companies.

  • Proof-of-life video. Verified 60 to 90 second videos of live operations.

  • Vetted deal flow. Opportunities pre-screened through bilateral trade chambers.

  • Business matching. Connect with partners across high-growth trade corridors.

Sectors
  • Critical minerals processing
  • Agri-tech
  • Green logistics
  • Digital services
